Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Swipe from a card to flip it in any of 8 directions. Find the one path that brings every card down to zero. The first levels include short tutorials, and new mechanics are explained the first time they appear.

I'm stuck on a level.

Use a hint to reveal the next step of the solution. You can also restart the level at any time.

How do I restore my purchases?

On iPhone and iPad, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. On Android, purchases are restored automatically when signed in with the same Google account.

How do I play a level someone shared with me?

Open the shared link on a device with FlipP4TH installed. The level opens in the game and is saved to your Community collection.

How do I stop analytics?

You can opt out of anonymous analytics at any time in the game's settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.
